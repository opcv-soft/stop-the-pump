# Player feedback form — GOD PUMP build {{BUILD}}

> Send with every build shared outside the studio (itch.io, Discord, friends). A bare "what do you think?" wastes the playtest.
> Answers go to `docs/feedback/` (one file per player, or a CSV export); QA reads them at the next playtest report.

1. How long did you play? When and why did you stop?
2. In one sentence, what is this game?
3. What was the best moment? (be specific)
4. What was the most frustrating or confusing moment?
5. Was there anything you tried to do that the game didn't let you do?
6. Rate 1–5: *[targeted form of fun #1, in player words — e.g. "I cared about him"]*
7. Rate 1–5: *[targeted form of fun #2]*
8. Rate 1–5: *[each pillar, in player words]*
9. Would you play again tomorrow? Why / why not?
10. What do you usually play? (helps us know if you're the target audience)
11. Device / OS / screen size — and did anything run slowly?
12. Anything else?
