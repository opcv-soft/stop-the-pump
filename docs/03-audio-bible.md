# GOD PUMP — Audio bible

> Owner: Audio Director. Written on 2026-10-31.

## Sonic identity
Plain SFX + looping background music. No music engineering unless the Director asks again (Director's call).

## Music
`assets/audio/music/roadside_at_two.mp3` ("Roadside At Two", ElevenLabs Music). Before release: confirm the plan allows commercial use.

## Feedback map
| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| Pouring | glug |
| Good fills / chain | applause from the regulars |
| Closing, coins into the register | register (ka-ching) |
| Delivery | truck, horn |
| Big reveal | choir |
Placeholders synthesized by `python3 tools/make_godpump_sounds.py` (applause, register, horn, choir, truck).
