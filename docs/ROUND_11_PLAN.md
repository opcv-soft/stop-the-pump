# GOD PUMP: round 11 build plan (2026-10-25)

> From playtest 12 (v0.47.0). "The report works." Biggest weaknesses: three upgrade layers that confuse,
> chaining that no longer matters, and moments (closing, the cash, new unlocks) that don't land.
> **DECIDED** marks a call the Committee made, to review at the playtest.

## Designer's calls (2026-10-25)
- **Keep the truck's cards:** "they create tons of tension". But they must be stronger than the tree's
  upgrades, or of another nature.
- **Scrap the permanent upgrades.** Papa's Garage becomes a second tab of the night screen, in the run.
- **Nozzles are straight improvements on one another.** Speed and area are the core attributes; each can have
  one extra perk.
- **Truck: full deliveries only**, except when the tank is dry (then whatever the cash buys).

## 11A: fixes and clarity
1. **The truck brings a full tank or nothing**, unless the tank is dry.
2. **Drones and the player on one car:** a car leaves only once it's past 80% and nothing is pouring into it.
   A drone no longer settles a car you're pouring on, and letting go of a car a drone is filling doesn't
   send it off.
3. **Closing pays everything, visibly:** at closing every coin on the lot (and the coins of the cars that
   leave under 80%, who pay for what they got) flies straight to the tray, then into the register.
4. **A big OUVERT sign swings in when the day opens, and flips to FERMÉ at closing.**
5. **The cash register reads as the cash:** a CASH label and the amount. The old pump reads as the tank: TANK
   and a percentage.
6. **The books, the accountant's way:** no negative numbers. Today's results lists revenue and costs, then
   the net profit (or net loss). The balance sheet shows liabilities as positive amounts. (The cousin's loan
   card goes, see 11B.)

## 11B: one run, two currencies
- **The night has two tabs: THE SHOP (cash) and THE GARAGE (this run's XP).** Nothing carries over between runs
  any more (Papa's Garage between runs and the meta save's XP are gone; the lifetime stats stay).
- **The nozzle ladder (Garage):** each nozzle replaces the last, faster and with a bigger ring, plus a perk:
  Le Classique (start) · Le Costaud · Le Chromé · Le Turbo · La Dorée.
- **The tree loses its speed and area nodes** (Bec large, Débit +): the nozzles own them now. MULTI-JET hangs off
  Visée auto I and drops Bec évasé.
- **Bling and perks (Garage):** bought once with XP, always on (no slots). The start-of-run perks go.
- **The cards become rule-changers (DECIDED):** fewer, stronger, each bends a rule. The plain stat cards go.
- XP is earned **and spent** in the run: chaining is how you pay for the Garage. The car ladder counts all the
  XP earned in the run, spent or not.

## Balance
- **The car ladder is faster** (thresholds about 2.5 times lower).
- **Harder to win:** reputation from fills down, Gaétan's push grows day by day (he upgrades too), the share moves
  fewer points a night. Target: an average player (the bot) rarely wins; a skilled one has to work for it.

## 11C: make chaining matter
- **The combo pill pops and grows** as the chain climbs, burns hotter, and shatters when it breaks.
- **The chain's value is on screen:** "1+2+3 = 6" while it runs; when it breaks, its value flies to the day's
  combo total.
- **A first-day tutorial** that teaches by doing: pour, stop at the top, the ring, chain good fills, what a
  chain is worth, the day bar, the night's two tabs.

## 11D: reveals that land
- **Closing like opening a pack:** the stacks pour into the register (ka-ching), then the report reveals itself
  piece by piece (the share bar sweeps, the numbers count up, each column pops in).
- **A new car type is a card reveal:** rays, the car spinning in, its name and rule.
