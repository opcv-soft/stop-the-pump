# GOD PUMP: the lore

> The world's bible. Round 10 batch B (2026-10-25, designer) replaced the "Dad faked his death to go
> fishing" story with the fight against Gaétan. The intro and the ending (scenes/god/ui/cinematic.gd),
> the visitors (scripts/god/visitors.gd) and Dad's lines on the day report (scenes/god/ui/day_report_screen.gd)
> all draw from it. Tone: tongue-in-cheek, warm, small-town Québec. English text, French names and quotes.

## The place
**Saint-Clin-Clin-des-Meuh-Meuh, Québec.** Population 412, and more cows than that (the Meuh-Meuh).
- **On the road:** a church with a silver steeple, the rang where the farms are, and the avenue.
- **The two stations:** your gaz bar, and **Chez Gaétan** right across the road.

## The story
- **Dad** was the best gas man in the village for thirty years. He topped off to the cent, every time.
  Saint-Clin-Clin never needed a second gas station: everybody filled up at his.
- **Then his hands started to shake.** Fills ran over, fills came up short.
- **Gaétan opened across the road.** Câlisse. Half the village started filling up there.
- **Dad tried hiring.** "Ces gars-là l'ont pas." (Those guys don't have it.)
- **So he hands you the keys** to the God Pump: "Win them back, kid. All of them."
- **The run:** every car on the avenue that wants gas picks you or Gaétan. Your market share starts at 50%.
  Good fills raise your reputation; the village follows your reputation, a few points a night; Gaétan's
  specials claw a little back every night.
- **The win: 100% of the village.** Gaétan turns off his sign. The village cheers, Ti-Guy cries. Dad gets up
  from the bench, hands steady for once: "To the cent, kid. TO THE CENT!" He laughs so hard his heart
  gives out. He dies the happiest gas man in Québec. Avenged.
- **The loss:** 0% (Gaétan has the whole village), or no gas and no money for the truck.

## The family
- **Dad (Papa):** alive, retired, shaky hands, a big heart (it matters at the end). Drives the old orange
  hatchback. Visits on days 3, 15 and 25; reacts to the market share every night on the day report.
- **You,** the kid. The town watches you learn the pump, and wonders if you're single.

## The regulars (the bench by the door)
They don't speak during the day any more (playtest 9): they walk, hop, cheer and applaud from the store's side.
- **Ti-Guy:** short, red plaid flannel, a cap, a moustache; a dairy farmer. Dad's loudest fan.
- **Mme Gagnon:** grey bun, glasses, a pink cardigan, a purse; the village gossip. Has a niece, Sylvie. She's very nice.
- **Bertrand:** bald, big belly, suspenders; a retired mechanic and Dad's best friend. Dry.
- **Le Grand:** very tall, a leather jacket, a mullet, a beer; plays in Les Pistons (church basement, Thursdays).

## Around town
- **Gaétan:** runs the station across the road. Smug, cheap, never topped off a thing in his life.
- **Me Tremblay, the notary:** Gaétan asks him to draw up buy-out papers. He'd rather not.
- **Cousin Réjean:** Dad tried to hire him once; he spilled on both of Dad's shoes. Works nights once you can pay him (HEURES).
- **The curé:** half his parish fills up across the road. He prays for both of you.
- **Pétroles St-Clin:** the fuel truck, number 31. Its prices only go up.

## Where the lore shows
- **The intro:** the best gas man in the village, the shaky hands, Gaétan, the keys.
- **The visitors (scripts/god/visitors.gd):** on set days someone drives in and chats with the regulars,
  wordless on the lot; the lines are read on the day report (OVERHEARD TODAY):
  - Gaétan (days 2, 18, 40), Papa (3, 15, 25), Sylvie (5, 21), cousin Réjean (7, 35), Me Tremblay (9, 30), the curé (12).
- **The day report:** the market share bar, the reputation, and one line from Dad.
- **The ending:** 100% market share.
